G1 · Watch History · Isolated concept

Clock hand moves on click

Click the Watch History icon in the top-right toolbar. The hands rotate 75° counterclockwise as Watch History opens, then return when you click it again. The circular return arrow, other icons and all toolbar button centers remain fixed.

Simulated Watch History state: Closed

Click the middle toolbar button to open the simulated Watch History view. Click it again to close. Repeated quick clicks reverse the movement naturally.

This is a standalone icon interaction trial, not an actual Watch History page. No application data or pages are modified.
