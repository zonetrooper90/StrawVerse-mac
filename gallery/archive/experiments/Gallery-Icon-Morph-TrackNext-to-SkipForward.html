Player Track Next → Player Skip Forward

Standalone morph experiment for Gallery. The forward 10-second button uses Player Track Next. In Play Next mode, it morphs into Player Skip Forward. The morph is reversible, interruptible, and the rest of the player layout stays anchored.

How to use

Click the forward button, use the slider, or hit the toggle button below. You can reverse the morph mid-transition to test how it behaves when crossing the last 20-second boundary back and forth.

What is morphing

Left triangle grows from the first triangle of Player Track Next into the larger triangle of Player Skip Forward.
Right triangle collapses into the vertical bar of Player Skip Forward.
Play Next UI fades in alongside the icon morph instead of appearing instantly.

This is still just the icon experiment. It does not change Gallery v97 or create a new player build.
